Methods & assumptions

Endpoints: ERα activation (estrogen receptor α reporter assay, specific endpoint); MMP (mitochondrial membrane potential disruption, mitotoxicity); Cytotoxicity (general cell viability, IC₁₀).

Calculation: Exposure concentrations in any unit are first converted to molar (M) using each substance's molecular weight. The relative effect potency REPi = EC₁₀BPA / EC₁₀i is dimensionless. BPA-EQi = ci(molar) × REPi, expressed in molar BPA-equivalents, then multiplied by MWBPA (228.291 g/mol) to give ng/L predicted BPA-EQ.

Concentration addition is the underlying assumption — substances are assumed to act via the same mode of action within each endpoint. For endpoints where this assumption is weaker (e.g. cytotoxicity), interpret BPA-EQ as a screening estimate.

Missing values mean no EC₁₀ could be derived in the tested concentration range for that assay. Such substances are dropped from the BPA-EQ sum for that endpoint, with a warning shown in the results.

How it works. Exposure concentrations are converted to molar units via each substance's molecular weight. The relative effect potency REPi = EC₁₀BPA / EC₁₀i (dimensionless, both in M) gives a BPA-equivalent molar concentration BPA-EQi = ci(M) × REPi. Final BPA-EQ mass concentration: BPA-EQi × MWBPA (228.291 g/mol). Total mixture BPA-EQ is the sum across substances, assuming concentration addition.
